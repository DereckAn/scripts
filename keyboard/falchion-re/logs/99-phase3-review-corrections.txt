BLOCKER 1 — function bodies were treated as contiguous, and many are not
FalchionFunctionInventory.java allocated body.getNumAddresses() bytes and
read them contiguously from the entry point; match_functions.py made the same
entry..entry+size assumption for body diffs and gap boundaries. Ghidra bodies
can be discontiguous and in this firmware many are, so the exact-body hashes,
the body-versus-data classification and the 21/446 body-byte totals in log 98
were all unsound.

Scale of the problem, from the regenerated inventories:

FIX 1
The inventory script now iterates body.getAddressRanges(true), reads each
real range, hashes the concatenation in address order, and emits the ordered
ranges as a new 'ranges=' field. match_functions.py parses those ranges,
refuses an inventory that lacks them, refuses one whose ranges do not sum to
the reported size, computes body diffs only when the two range *shapes*
match, and derives data spans from the complement of the union of real
ranges. All four inventories were regenerated and every number recomputed.
