After being told that the next phase was one 48-byte flash block at address

OFFLINE PREPARATION
Added tool/probe_flash_read.py, whose exact allowlist permits only:
  - length 0x30;
  - address 0x00010000;
  - exactly one execute-READ report 1f 05;
  - status and data queries needed for bootstrap/freshness.

It cannot construct another address, a second execute-READ, unlock, erase,
program, reset, or update. Unlike the full backup handshake, this first probe
does not re-arm EXEC: it sends exactly one execute-READ and waits boundedly for
the buffer to change and for the following status to prove it is outside the
transfer interval. It then performs the confirming fetch.

INTERPRETATION
The bootloader accepted exactly one execute-READ at address 0x10000 and length
0x30. The sample/status/confirm ordering proved the returned buffer was complete
and newer than the all-zero bootstrap. It begins with the expected SN_FWIN
container magic.

The installed and preserved-vendor 48-byte blocks match at offsets 0x00..0x2b.
The final u32 record-checksum field differs:
  installed: 85 24 55 7d
  vendor 1.00.58: 7a c1 75 5e
This is evidence that the installed record payload differs from the preserved
1.00.58 image. The shared `v1.0.00` container string is not the keyboard's
bcdDevice firmware release.

FINAL SAFETY ACCOUNTING
Exactly one flash execute-READ was sent, for 48 bytes at 0x10000. No other
address, second execute-READ, unlock, erase, program, reset after entry, update,
driver detach, or SPI command occurred. The read returned data only; no firmware
or persistent configuration was changed. No full backup was attempted.
