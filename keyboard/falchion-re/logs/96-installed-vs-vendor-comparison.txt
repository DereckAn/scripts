DESIGN
  * All structure parsing, offset translation and checksum policy come from
    the Phase-1 falchion_image module. The comparator adds no second SN_FWIN
    parser and no second copy of the offset rules.
  * The installed dump covers logical [0x10000,0x7c000), so the vendor file is
    read over the same logical range through ImageView.index. Byte 0 is never
    compared against byte 0.
  * Both inputs must be allowlisted sources. --analysis-only lifts that after
    printing a warning that the results must not be cited as evidence about
    the shipped firmware. The comparator implements this refusal itself rather
    than calling require_supported_source, which is the mutation gate.
  * Markdown and JSON render one model. The JSON is complete; the Markdown
    tables the 25 longest differing ranges and says so.

FACTS ESTABLISHED IN THIS PHASE
  1. Over logical [0x10000,0x7c000) the two images differ in 101,297 of
     442,368 bytes (22.90%), in 3,509 contiguous ranges, touching 39 of 108
     0x1000 pages. First difference 0x1002c, last range ends at 0x7c000.
  2. Every change is confined to three runs: [0x10000,0x17000),
     [0x21000,0x40000) and [0x7b000,0x7c000). Everything else is identical,
     including the whole container/bootloader-copy area [0x60000,0x71000),
     the 0xff fill [0x17000,0x21000), the zero fill [0x40000,0x60000) and
     [0x71000,0x74000) and [0x79000,0x7b000), and the RAM image
     [0x74000,0x79000).
  3. Record slot 0 keeps its length 0x58ac and differs in 131 bytes across
     106 ranges. Record slot 1 grows from 0x1e754 to 0x1e780 (+44) and
     differs in 101,112 bytes across 3,399 ranges over the common prefix.
     Neither record moved: both remain at 0x60011000 and 0x60021000.
  4. The installed mirrored bootloader copy [0x61000,0x71000) is identical to
     both the vendor backup range and the vendor primary region [0,0x10000),
     with zero differing ranges in either comparison.
  5. All 603 ASCII strings of six or more printable bytes are present in both
     images. Nothing was added, removed, or rewritten. The changed regions
     therefore carry no plain-text string differences at all.
  6. Both images independently satisfy their own known structural checks and
     word sums: application 0x2d7486db installed against 0x5d27c5a9 vendor,
     bootloader_mirror 0xfb665ae3 in both.

ASSUMPTIONS AND DELIBERATE LIMITS
  * No function, field or behavior is inferred from any changed range. The
     report states this and repeats the three open UNRESOLVED items.
  * Record diff ranges cover the common prefix only. Slot 1's extra 44 bytes
    have no counterpart in the vendor image and are reported as a length
    delta, not as a difference.
  * The comparator refuses a record slot that is active in only one image, or
    a record whose address moved, instead of guessing a correspondence.
  * String comparison uses ASCII runs of at least six printable bytes, drops
    substrings of longer hits, and pairs a removal with an addition at difflib
    ratio >= 0.8 so a rewrite is reported once. It is a text-level signal and
    cannot see changes inside compressed or non-ASCII data.
  * Region classification is per-0x1000 page and collapsed into runs. A run
    marked unchanged means every page in it is byte-identical.
  * The Markdown note tables the 25 longest differing ranges and says so; the
    JSON carries all 3,509 plus all 108 page hashes.
  * Phase 1 was corrected under independent review in log 95 and had not been
    formally accepted when this phase was executed at the owner's direction.
